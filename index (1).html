HIDE FROM MATHEW

Escape the endless rooms. Don’t get caught.

HOW TO PLAY

Collect enough map pieces (white sheets) to complete the map, then walk into the green exit. Each level needs 10 more maps than the last.

WASD move · Mouse or arrow keys look · Shift sprint · C crouch · Space throw a flare (scares Mathews away) · Hold E to fix a generator (+5 maps, but the noise attracts Mathews).

You have 3 lives. If a Mathew catches you, you lose a life and the Mathews are thrown back for a few seconds. Lose all 3 and the level is over.

Three kinds of Mathew: Regular, Spider (crawls on the ceiling and drops on you) and Disguiser (pretends to be a map piece, but his maps glow red).

MULTIPLAYER

Everyone with the same room code plays the same maze, sees each other, and shares map pieces and generators. Each player is hunted by their own Mathews. The first person in a room is the host, so they should keep their tab open.
